This project was suggested by a Hackaday article from February 2025 in which the developer created a handheld yagi for the 868 MHz European Meshtastic mode and used it for range testing on those devices. The team at the Gloucester County (NJ) Amateur Radio Club recognized the potential opportunity to redesign the antenna for the 915 MHz US band and create a window-mounted antenna for operating that would direct the RF outside of the building towards other stations. This would be far more effective than the simple antennas that come with the Meshtastic radios.

Chris Prioi AD2CS took on the project and remodeled the antenna for 915 MHZ, redesigned the 3D printed boom for the higher frequency and added mounts at each end for a suction cup that mounts on the inside or outside of the window. He also added a matching inductor to bring the match closer to 50 ohms and wrote detailed assembly instructions.
